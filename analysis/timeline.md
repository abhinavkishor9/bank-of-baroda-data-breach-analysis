# Incident Timeline

## Overview

This timeline summarizes publicly reported events related to the Bank of Baroda cybersecurity incident.

It is **not** an attacker timeline or forensic reconstruction. The sequence below is based on publicly available information and should not be interpreted as confirmation of attacker activity.

---

| Date / Stage | Event | Status |
|--------------|-------|--------|
| Initial Incident | An employee's corporate email account was reportedly compromised. | ✅ Publicly Reported |
| Following Discovery | Claims emerged that approximately **3 TB** of organizational data had been exposed on a dark web forum. | ✅ Publicly Reported |
| Bank Response | Bank of Baroda stated that its **Core Banking System (CBS)** and customer transaction processing services were **not affected**. | ✅ Publicly Confirmed |
| Investigation Initiated | A forensic investigation was launched to determine the scope, impact, and root cause of the incident. | ✅ Publicly Reported |
| Investigation Phase | Security teams began assessing whether the compromise extended beyond the employee email account. | 🔍 Under Investigation |
| Attack Path Validation | Investigators would analyze identity, endpoint, cloud, network, and data access evidence to reconstruct the attacker lifecycle. | 🔍 Investigation Methodology |
| Incident Assessment | Final conclusions regarding attacker movement, affected systems, and data exposure depend on forensic evidence and have not been publicly confirmed. | 🔍 Ongoing Assessment |

---

# Timeline Interpretation

The publicly available information confirms the compromise of an employee email account and the initiation of a forensic investigation.

However, the progression from the initial compromise to the reported exposure of organizational data remains unconfirmed. Determining the complete attack chain requires evidence correlation across multiple security domains, including:

- Identity and authentication logs
- Email security logs
- Endpoint telemetry
- Network activity
- Cloud audit logs
- Data access records

---

# Key Observation

The employee email compromise represents the **confirmed initial access event**, but it should **not** be assumed to represent the complete attack chain.

A complete timeline of attacker activity can only be established through forensic investigation and validated evidence rather than publicly available reporting.
